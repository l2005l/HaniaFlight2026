## Full description
HaniaFlight is a fighter-jet simulator with a physics flight model and a full cockpit. The game is in Hebrew.

Four aircraft: F-15I Ra'am, F-16I Sufa, F-35I Adir and F-15 Baz, each with its own performance, weapons and switch panel.

Missions: strike, air defence against drones and cruise missiles, SAM suppression, armed reconnaissance against a convoy, strike escort, combat search and rescue, photo reconnaissance with a targeting pod, a stealthy low-level drone, air combat against MiG-29, Su-27 or MiG-21, air-to-air refuelling and guided lessons.

Also: a full cold start from a hardened shelter with ground crew; two linked campaigns, one keeping a fleet and a weapons stock between sorties; a daily challenge; three theatres; day, dusk and night with night-vision goggles, runway lighting and PAPI; crosswind, storms and morning fog; failures such as flame-outs with air restarts, brake failure with an arresting cable, and bird strikes; a wingman who takes orders and lands with you in formation; replays, photo mode, a pilot profile with ranks and achievements; touch, tilt or game-controller controls.

No ads, no purchases, no data collection: the logbook, achievements and settings stay on the device.
